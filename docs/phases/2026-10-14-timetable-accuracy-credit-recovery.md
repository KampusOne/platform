# Complete timetable extraction and failed-import credit recovery

The reported UNIBEN engineering timetable mixed 100-level Wednesday courses with 200-level courses and assigned GST212 and CVE211 to earlier time columns. The previous validator accepted any remaining valid rows after dropping invalid ones. Weekly import reservations also survived failed inference, so a fresh retry spent another allowance.

Class-table extraction now transcribes ordered time-column headers, explicit weekday/level rows and merged cell spans, including empty columns. The Worker derives clocks from those headers, validates coverage of every selected row and rejects mixed levels, missing days, gaps and overlaps. The stored student level supplies the default; an explicit level selector overrides it. Optional course codes filter transcribed alternatives without changing their printed times. Laboratories keep their full duration. Parenthesised department markers are not treated as rooms.

Class lists, calendars and exams must report complete row coverage. Truncated output, uncertain or omitted rows and invalid required fields fail the whole scan; no partial review is presented. Changing the source or selection clears the old review, and a failed retry persists its new request key and uploaded attachment. Receipt polling continues to check the same request after a lost response.

Failed and abandoned AI requests no longer count towards weekly source-import usage. The next reservation removes their allowance rows under the existing per-user quota lock, then checks fresh counts in the same transaction. Live and completed reservations still count once; attempt audit records remain intact. This repairs past failed reservations without a database migration.
