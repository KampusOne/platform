Class-table extraction now transcribes ordered time-column headers, explicit weekday/level rows and merged cell spans, including empty columns. For dense multi-level ruled images, the Worker detects physical horizontal and vertical borders in bounded private JPEG/PNG pixels, isolates the selected level's cells and sends numbered panels for transcription. Each day read must include the printed level label, all cells and every blank. The model cannot choose the row, move a cell or shorten its span. The Worker derives clocks from those headers, validates coverage of every selected row and rejects mixed levels, missing days, gaps and overlaps. Unsupported or ambiguous image geometry fails without an import charge. The stored student level supplies the default; an explicit level selector overrides it. Optional course codes filter transcribed alternatives without changing their printed times. Laboratories keep their full duration. Parenthesised department markers are not treated as rooms.

The first dense-image read uses a compact contact sheet containing all time headings and day/level labels, with a 30-second attempt limit and one bounded transient-failure retry. It does not contain course cells. All later cell crops come from the complete original private source and share its overall 150-second deadline and single quota reservation. This avoids a whole-image layout stall without asking the student to split their timetable.

Class lists, calendars and exams must report complete row coverage. Truncated output, uncertain or omitted rows and invalid required fields fail the whole scan; no partial review is presented.
