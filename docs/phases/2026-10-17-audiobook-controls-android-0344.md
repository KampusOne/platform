# Audiobook controls and Android 0.3.44

The audiobook screen uses the live Expo audio player with a seek bar, elapsed/total time, play/pause, bounded 15-second skips, repeat and four playback speeds. Multiple audio parts also expose previous/next chapter buttons with disabled boundaries. Controls have at least 44 px targets and fit a 320 px phone. Native uses Expo's compatible community slider; web uses a keyboard-accessible range input. Audio streams from the existing short-lived authorized URL without a download, save or share control. This UI does not claim to prevent extraction of a stream by a device owner.

Unloaded controls stay disabled. Loading or buffering that stalls for 15 seconds exposes retry, which obtains a fresh authorized link through the screen's existing recovery path. Failed seeks show a recoverable error; concurrent actions are guarded, replay seeks to zero, and the hook disposes the old player when changing chapter or leaving.

Campus brand names ending in Vendor, Tutor or Rider place that role on the final label row. The existing trusted seal is 11 px with a 3 px gap beside the role. Other names retain their full two-line label and compact adjacent seal. Only explicitly verified accounts receive the seal; the combined photo/name profile target remains intact.
