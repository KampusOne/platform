# Legal publication checklist — KampusOne

**Status: DRAFT, NOT APPROVED FOR PUBLICATION (12 October 2026).**

The expanded in-app Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions provide feature-specific detail, but the following details are not established in the supplied product documentation. The operator must verify and a qualified Nigerian legal professional must review them before removing the on-screen draft notice or publishing the text as a binding policy:

- Exact legal entity/controller name, registered or service address, and reliable data protection contact.
- Accurate current list of data processors/subprocessors, cross-border data destinations, contractual safeguards and payment roles.
- Retention schedule by data category, including verification files, private chats, AI history, analytics, financial evidence and device caches; deletion workflows.
- Legitimate-interest assessments and consent flows for sensitive data, marketing, under-18 agents, photos and optional location sharing.
- Precise agent eligibility and guardian approval policy; university-level identity verification and escalation paths.
- Actual current marketplace commission, refund, cancellation, subscription renewal, free-trial and dispute processes, without contradicting statutory consumer rights.
- Operational support contact, response commitments, legal notices, redress procedures and approved effective date.
- Security control descriptions verified against deployed infrastructure rather than desired architecture.
- Explicit in-app accessibility, cross-links and confirmation of policy versions stored with any consent or acceptance event.

The app's legal screen intentionally marks the text as a draft while these items are open. Do not remove that label merely to make the UI appear finished.

References for Nigerian rights: NDPC (Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, data-subject rights); FCCPC (consumer rights under FCCPA Part XV). These reference sources do not constitute legal advice.

This legal work is separate from the production troubleshooting of payments, AI imports, and performance. No operational claims about those systems are proven merely by writing policy copy.
